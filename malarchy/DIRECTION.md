# Malarchy wallpaper direction

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal computer as a 1990s cut-and-paste magazine cover. The user explicitly selected this world and real Sudeikis-as-Biden photography; no AI art.

OWN-WORLD: Catppuccin Mocha ground and text, personal purple/cyan/pink/mint/yellow paper, Bowlby One SC cut letters, photocopied portrait with a hand-traced white edge, typewriter slogan strips. Normal desktop controls retain Catppuccin.

STORY: Recognize Malarchy, read the ownership tagline, smile at the pointing Biden. This is a static wallpaper with no controls or motion, not a web application.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440×900 composition rendered at 2× for the MacBook's 2880×1800 display. Left half: oversized two-line wordmark, pronunciation, slogan. Right half: portrait over purple, crossing cyan strip. Bottom: personal-computer sticker and edition credit. Static composition is the signature; no animation runs behind work.

FORM: User-pinned magazine collage overrides alternate concept directions; concept seed 9cc37535 was run and acknowledged. Terminal, risograph and folding-paper challengers cannot replace the explicitly selected material language. From the terminal: readability; from risograph: committed spot colors; from folding paper: clear overlapping layers. No generated comp exists because the user explicitly rejected AI art.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
